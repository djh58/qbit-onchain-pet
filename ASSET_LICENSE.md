# Asset license

The original Qbit mascot files in `assets/qbit/` and their favicon and social-card
derivatives in `site/public/` are made available under the repository's MIT
license.

The `assets/miner-one/` spritesheet is an AI-assisted derivative of the original
Qbit mascot. An image-generation concept established the gold hardhat, `#1`
badge, and pickaxe direction; `scripts/build-miner-one-sprite.mjs` redraws those
accessories deterministically on the canonical logical pixel grid. The tracked
lossless WebP is the exact asset inscribed on Qbit mainnet in transaction
`53dd2d8982bea1dd1676f6506a497a31c14a81aad21639d019e3658150f231af`.

The Qbit name and logo identify the Qbit project. The MIT copyright license does
not grant trademark rights or permission to imply endorsement by Qbit or its
contributors.

`site/public/og.png` is an AI-assisted social-card derivative. Its generated
source carried C2PA provenance metadata, but the optimized public PNG does not
retain that metadata. Neither file contains a private prompt, account
credential, or wallet data.
